1Resizable, with zoom

Nova resizes:

  • drag any edge or corner;
  • the layout reflows instead of scaling;
  • a zoom chip makes the text bigger: 85, 100, 120 or 140%;
  • Nova remembers both the size and the zoom.

2A signal path you can see

The tab row and the single big blue card are replaced by a strip running OSC A → B → C → SUB/NOISE → FILTERS → FX → VOICES → OUT. Each tile shows a live thumbnail, and its light shows whether that part is on.

Click a tile to open that part, with its controls beside it and related parts below. The mod sources stay docked on every page. The panel turns graphite, and blue is kept for what's active.

3A browser that doesn't eat your keys

The cause: the browser puts the cursor in its search box when it opens, so letters type there instead of playing notes.

The fix: the browser now opens with the list selected, so your keys keep playing and ↑/↓ auditions presets.

  • / or a click puts you in search, and the pill turns amber;
  • Esc brings you back to playing;
  • the browser docks to the side instead of covering the panel.

4✦ Constellation: browse by sound, then morph

Every preset becomes a star, placed by how it actually sounds. Nova renders each one and measures its brightness, envelope, movement and width; the names don't matter.

Click between stars and the sound you hear is a blend of its neighbours, with every knob in between. Drag while you hold a chord and it morphs live.

  • Mutate scatters six variations around you;
  • Keep mine holds your FX, envelopes or routes while the rest morphs;
  • Keep this saves the blend as a new star.
