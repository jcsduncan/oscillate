Everything on one page

The oscillators, sub and noise, both filters, routing, modulation and macros are all visible together, so you can see what drives what while you tweak.

Hierarchy, not hiding

Each card has its main knobs at full size. Secondary controls are smaller and grouped under a label. Parts that are switched off fade, and each part keeps its own colour.

Open a part when you want it

Press ⤢, or double-click a card's title, to open that part large over the page with everything else still behind it. Esc goes back. Resize and zoom give every card more room at once.

Slim strip

The signal path stays at the top as a thin strip with lights and small pictures. Click a name to jump to its card. FX, MATRIX, ARP · CLIP, VOICES and GLOBAL are page buttons in the header.

Compared with what's built now

The rebuild goes back to the first build's all-in-one page and keeps everything else from the current one: the colours, graphite, key strip, side browser, status line and Constellation.
