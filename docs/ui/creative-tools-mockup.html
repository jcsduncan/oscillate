Creative tools: Arp · Groove · Arrange

Three tools for the Funky project (126 BPM, A minor): a Nova arp rebuilt as a pattern engine, grooves taken from records into the groove pool, and a whole arrangement proposed from a loop that you keep or bin in one step. Click a Play button to hear it. Every random choice is seeded, so the same settings always play the same.

The arp page of Nova. Sixteen modes in six groups, sixty factory patterns, and ten step lanes, each with its own length, so a 16-step velocity lane, a 12-step gate lane and a 7-step note lane drift against each other and line up again only every 336 steps. Grooves come from the pool, including ones taken from records. Hold a chord on the keyboard or with the typing keys (A W S E D F T G Y H U J K, Z/X for octave). The patterns list is focused, so the keys play. Press / to search and Esc to go back.

Take the timing and accents from a record, or from an audio clip in the project, and save them to the groove pool as an ordinary groove template. It uses the beat grid we already have (tempo and key analysis, ADR-0052), finds onsets in one band, and averages every 16th step over the bars you choose: how late or early it lands (in ms and as a share of a step) and how loud it is. The result works anywhere a groove does: MIDI clips, the Nova arp, Chop and Knock.

Select a loop and get a full, DJ-friendly arrangement. It works out each track's role from its instrument and its analysis, places sections on 8, 16 and 32-bar boundaries with something new every 16 bars, writes the automation (filter sweeps, a high-pass before the drop, reverb and delay throws, Chop fills) and keeps the intro and outro as beats only for mixing. The proposal is one changeset: Keep applies it as a single undo step, and Bin leaves the project as it was. Drag a section edge, lock a track, or regenerate for a new seed.

A deterministic rule engine for now. The same loop, template and seed always give the same arrangement, and every rule is listed above. Later, an LLM agent uses the same tools: Ears (roles, energy and the beat grid) and Hands (session commands for clip copies, mutes and automation). Its proposals arrive the same way, as a changeset you keep or bin.
