Deep Chords

Progressions as melodic techno, Anjunadeep, Ben Böhmer-style progressive and trance use them, voiced the way those records sound: triads with add9 and sus2 colour, open fifths and octaves in the left hand, a note ringing on top, and no jazz pull. Pick a progression, then flip Voicing to hear the difference. Lush 9ths is what the DAW writes today.
