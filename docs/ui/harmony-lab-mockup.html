Harmony Lab

A Chord track under the ruler becomes the song's harmony. Comp plays it in a groove, Bass follows it, Melody writes against it and Living clips change on every pass. Press Play and change anything: what you hear updates on the next step.

This is the design for ADR-0083. The shipped harmony tools follow ADR-0084: chords are notes in MIDI clips (see chord-clips-mockup.html). The Chord track, Melody, Living clips and Render to clip are not built.

Comp is a note effect that plays the Chord track in a rhythm, on any synth. Each voice of the chord has its own lane: the low note, the middle notes and the top note. The top lane can run at its own length against the bar. Pushes bring the next chord in a 16th or 8th early, the house anticipation. Click a cell to cycle it: on, ghost, off.
