One curve, both directions

Each band has a single threshold. Above it the ratio pushes loud parts down (or expands them up); below it a second ratio lifts quiet parts (upward compression) or pushes them down (expansion). Drag the threshold node and the two handles on the curve, or use the knobs. Range caps the change either way.

Kick-aware low band

The low band can key from the Anvil kick through the existing sidechain sends. Because Anvil's curves are known ahead, the engine can start ducking on the kick's first sample with no lookahead, the same source Kick & Bass Lock and Pump read.

Crossovers

  • Minimum phase: Linkwitz–Riley 24 dB/oct, zero latency beyond lookahead, bands sum flat in level.
  • Linear phase: FIR crossovers, no phase shift between bands, about 21 ms more latency. For the master and buses.
  • Crossovers stay a third of an octave apart. The preview here always plays minimum phase.

What the panel draws

  • Faint: the spectrum going in. Bright: coming out.
  • Coloured fills under the 0 dB line: each band's live gain change in its shape. Orange-tinted cuts down, green lifts up.
  • On the curve, the moving dot is where the band's level is now.
