Chop

A tempo-locked gate and stutter for bass. Draw the steps, give any step ratchets (2–4 hits inside it) or a repeat (grab that slice of the bass and replay it, pitching down as it goes), set a chance per step for variation that is the same on every playback and export, then swing, rotate and shape the edges. Press Play and change anything while it loops.

124 BPM · 1 bar · F minor bass
Gate and repeat in oneGate shapes the level step by step. Repeat captures the bass at a step's start and replays that slice (1/16 to 1/64), dropping in pitch and fading as it goes, like a beat-repeat fill. Use both for chops that stutter.
Chance you can count onEach step's chance rolls from a seed tied to the bar, so a 60% step plays the same bars every time, live and in an export. Re-seed for a new variation.
No clicksEvery edge is a short equal-power fade (Smooth), even on 1/64 ratchets, and repeats crossfade into the dry signal. Mix blends with the dry bass for parallel chopping.
