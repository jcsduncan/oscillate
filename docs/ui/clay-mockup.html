Clay — the notes inside your audio

Double-click an audio clip and open NOTES. Clay finds every note — one voice, a stacked chord or a run of hits — and lets you move it like MIDI: its pitch, wobble, drift, formant, level and timing. Then it does what only a DAW that owns its synths can: send the notes to Nova, harmonise in key, fit a sample to the song's key and chord, follow the bassline and chop out seeded variations.

HISTORY

    THREE WAYS TO HEAR

    Melodic follows one voice — a vocal, a bass, a lead. Polyphonic pulls a chord stab, a piano or a pad apart into stacked notes you can move one at a time. Percussive turns hits into short notes at their pitch centre. Detection runs in the background and is cached with the clip.

    NOTHING IS DESTROYED

    Every edit is a step on the clip: pitch, modulation, drift, formant, level and time per note. A/B plays the untouched audio; History goes back to any step. What you hear while playing is what the export renders.

    THE TOOLS

    1 Main moves pitch and time; double-click splits. 2 Pitch centre, 3 modulation, 4 drift, 5 formant, 6 amplitude, 7 time edges, 8 note separation: click splits, Shift-click joins, and in Polyphonic Alt-click says “that's an overtone” and gives its energy to the note below.

    WHY IT'S OURS

    Melodyne lives in a plug-in window. Clay knows the song: its key, its bassline, its groove pool and its synths — so notes go straight to Nova, a sample fits the song's chord, and a vocal chop can follow the bass.
