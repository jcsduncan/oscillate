# ADR-0082 — Strata: multiband dynamics

**Status:** Accepted; implemented. **Date:** 1 November 2026.
**Builds on:** ADR-0040 (native effect platform), ADR-0043 (one native insert path), ADR-0047 (Press), ADR-0062 (denormals flushed), ADR-0065 (Nova's Multiband module), ADR-0072 (Kick & Bass Lock).
**Panel design:** [ui/strata-mockup.html](../ui/strata-mockup.html).
**Contract:** `include/oscillate/native_dsp/effects/strata.hpp`, the table `src/native_dsp/effects/strata_table.hpp`, the shared crossovers `src/native_dsp/dsp/crossover.hpp`.

## Context

There was no multiband dynamics effect for tracks, buses or the master: Press is one band, Temper's dynamic bands are bells and shelves, Vista's bands only set width, and Nova's Multiband (OTT) module only works inside Nova. The jobs it does in minimal techno and house are a bass whose notes are uneven in the low end, kick and bass fighting under the crossover, a drum bus glued without the hats pumping, harsh hats, quiet details lifted, mud in stacked pads, the OTT sound, and a gentle master.

## Decision

1. **A native effect, Strata (id `strata`),** on the effect platform: a parameter table and a kernel, no document, with a sidechain.
2. **Bands.** One to six bands (`strata.bands`), split at up to five crossovers (`strata.xover_1..5`), kept ascending, a third of an octave apart and under 0.45 of the rate by the engine (`layout_for`), however the parameters are set.
   - **Minimum phase** (default): a tree of fourth-order Linkwitz-Riley splits built from RBJ Butterworth sections. Band k then passes the RBJ allpass of every higher crossover, so every band carries the same total allpass and the bands sum to it: flat magnitude at zero latency (measured within 0.1 dB, 20 Hz–20 kHz, six bands). The tree is `dsp::CrossoverTree` in the shared `crossover.hpp`; Vista and Nova keep their own older trees for now.
   - **Linear phase:** each band's FIR is the difference of two Blackman-windowed sinc low-passes (2047 taps at 48 kHz), the first band a low-pass and the last the delay less a low-pass, so the bands sum exactly to a delay of 1023 frames. They run by overlap-save on 512-frame hops in 4096-point FFTs, left and right as one complex signal, one inverse transform per band. Latency 1023 + 512 frames (32 ms at 48 kHz); all three sizes double per doubling of the rate. Below about 200 Hz a crossover's slope is gentler than the minimum-phase one (the FIR's length); the sum is still exact.
3. **One curve per band** (`gain_change_db`): above the threshold the slope is 1/above (above > 1 compresses, < 1 expands up), below it 1/below (below > 1 lifts quiet sound, < 1 pushes it down), blended over the knee by a quadratic, never more than the range either way; makeup after. The detector is the instantaneous level of the band's channel (the louder of left and right for stereo); the gain change itself is smoothed, with attack when the gain falls and release when it rises, so attack and release are exact time constants. A lift fades in from −84 to −64 dB of the band's falling peak (50 ms), so silence never rises into hiss, as in Nova's Multiband.
4. **Per band:** threshold, above, below, knee, range, attack, release, makeup; process (stereo, mid, side, left, right: the gain is applied to that part only, the rest passes untouched); key (the band, the whole input, or the sidechain split by its own minimum-phase tree into the same band; without a sidechain the band keys itself); solo, mute and bypass, which glide over 20 ms.
5. **Global:** input and output gain, mix (parallel, against the dry bands summed, so it is delay- and allpass-compensated and never combs), lookahead 0–20 ms (the audio is delayed against the detectors), auto gain (the dry and wet power over 3 s, within ±18 dB), delta (what the dynamics change, per heard band: delta + output = the dry bands).
6. **Latency** is lookahead plus the linear bank. Phase and lookahead are fixed per kernel and not automatable: a change rebuilds the route (`latency_frames`). Mode, band count, process and key are not automatable either.
7. **OTT mode** (`strata.mode`): three bands at 88 Hz and 2.5 kHz with thresholds −26, −28 and −32 dB, above 1 + 6·down, below 1 + 3·up, attack (1 + 29·time) ms and release (40 + 360·time) ms scaled 1.6/1/0.7 low to high, 6 dB knee, 24 dB range, makeup 1.5 dB·down, where Up / Down leans down and up. Depth, Time and Up / Down are automatable. `ott_values` writes it out as band parameters; the panel does that on the first touch of a band, so the sound does not change (tested: identical renders).
8. **Kick-aware low band:** the panel's button keys band 1 from the sidechain; route the kick's send to Strata. Kick & Bass Lock's kick profiles are internal to Anvil and Nova (ADR-0072) and not exposed to effects, so this is sidechain only, not ahead of the kick.
9. **Learn** (`strata.learn`): while on, each band's falling peak is binned for four seconds (0.5 dB from −90 dB); the suggestion is 4 dB under the level the band exceeds a quarter of the time. Telemetry carries progress and suggestions; the panel writes them as thresholds and turns Learn off in one edit.
10. **Telemetry:** peaks in and out, keyed, latency, auto gain, Learn, band count, per band its gain change, level and suggestion, the crossovers as they play, and a 96-band input and output spectrum (one 2048-point FFT 30 times a second for both, falling 36 dB/s).
11. **Presets** (from the design): Init (four bands), Glue the drums, Tame the bass (two low bands keyed), Air on the hats (upward), OTT 30%, Master polish (five bands, linear phase), De-mud the pad (a mid-only band).
12. **Panel** (`qml/strata`): the spectrum with band tints, input and output traces, each band's gain change drawn in the band's magnitude shape (`strata_band_shapes`), draggable crossovers and thresholds, double-click to add a band and × to remove one (the removed band joins the one below); a tab per band with solo, mute and bypass; the selected band's transfer curve from the engine (`strata_transfer`) with threshold, above and below handles and a live dot; the band's knobs, process and key; the OTT card; global controls, auto gain and auto threshold.

## Consequences

- **Tests:** `native_strata_contract` (crossover sums, the static curve against the formula and the soft knee, attack and release time constants, range, mid/side, external key, delta, latency, block splits, hostile input, OTT as its bands, no allocation, telemetry) and `quick_strata_panel_contract` (every parameter has a control, the curve, adding and removing bands, OTT take-over, telemetry and Learn, presets; screenshots with `STRATA_PANEL_SCREENSHOT_DIR`). `quick_native_effect_app_contract` covers it in the app.
- **CPU:** minimum phase costs a few biquads per band; linear phase about 7 FFTs of 4096 points per 512 frames.
- **Later:** move Vista and Nova's Multiband onto `crossover.hpp`; expose kick profiles to effects so the low band can duck ahead of the kick.
