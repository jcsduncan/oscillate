# ADR-0075 — Chop: a tempo-locked gate and stutter

**Status:** Accepted; implemented. **Date:** 30 October 2026.
**Builds on:** ADR-0040 (native effect platform), ADR-0043 (one native insert path), ADR-0058 (Pump: tempo cycles and swing).
**Panel design:** [ui/chop-mockup.html](../ui/chop-mockup.html).

## Context

Minimal techno and house chop the bass on the grid: a gate that opens on some sixteenths and not others, ratchets that roll into a beat, a beat-repeat that grabs a slice and stutters it, falling in pitch, as a fill. Pump draws one cycle of volume; it has no steps, no per-step ratchets or repeats, and no variation between bars. The design: steps on lanes (level, length, ratchet, repeat, chance), a gate drawn over the bass, Smooth, Shape, Swing, Rotate and the repeat's pitch and decay.

Two things have to hold for this to be usable in a mix: an export sounds as playback did, and no edge clicks however short it is.

## Decision

1. **A native effect, Chop (id `chop`),** on the effect platform: a parameter table and a kernel, no document, no sidechain, no latency.
2. **The pattern.** 16 or 32 steps of 1/8, 1/16 or 1/32 (`chop.steps`, `chop.division`), so 16 sixteenths is one bar. Each step has:
   - **Level** and **Length** (the gate's share of the step, or of each ratchet);
   - **Ratchet**, 1 to 4 hits inside the step;
   - **Repeat**, off or 2, 4 or 8 replays of the step's first half, quarter or eighth;
   - **Chance**, the probability it plays.

   **Swing** delays every even step by up to half a step (50 % to 75 % of the pair); **Rotate** plays step `(k − rotate) mod steps` in slot `k`.
3. **Modes.** *Gate* gates each step to its level. *Repeat* stutters the steps that have a repeat and passes every other step untouched. *Gate + repeat* gates the stutter too. Switching mode fades over Smooth.
4. **Edges.** Each hit opens at its start, moving the gain from where it is to the step's level along a raised cosine over Smooth (0.5–10 ms, default 2.9). After its Length it lets go over the release: Smooth × (1 + 12 × Shape), its curve bending from the same S (Hard) towards a fast drop with a long tail (Soft). A new edge always starts from the gain now, so an edge cut short never jumps. A gate stays open at least one Smooth, so it reaches its level before letting go, even on 1/128 ratchets. Edges start where they fall between frames, to the sample. Depth sets how far a closed step goes (1 is silence); Mix blends with the input.
   - The gate's edges are raised cosines, not the equal-power sine, because the gate scales one signal: a raised cosine has no corner for the product with the audio to turn into a click. Crossfades between two different signals (a repeat and the input, or one repeat and the next) are equal-power (sin/cos).
5. **Repeats, with no latency.**
   - A repeat step captures its input from its first frame into one of two buffers (they alternate per repeat step). The first slice plays live while it is captured; every later slice replays the capture from the step's start.
   - Slice `q` of `n` plays at rate `2^(−pitch · q / (n − 1) / 12)`, so the last is Repeat Pitch semitones down. Its level is `1 − decay · q / (n − 1)`. Reads go through a four-point Lagrange interpolator.
   - Each slice crossfades from the one before over Smooth (at most half a slice). The step's last slice crossfades into the next step the same way.
   - A step entered part way (after a seek) cannot replay its start, so it plays through.
   - **Limit:** each buffer holds half the longest step, a fully swung 1/8 at 20 BPM (`chop::min_repeat_bpm`), plus the widest crossfade: 1.135 s, 54,484 frames at 48 kHz, two stereo float buffers (0.87 MB). A slice that does not fit (only below 20 BPM) plays through. Allocated when the kernel is created; the callback never allocates.
6. **Determinism.**
   - The song position is anchored. At a control tick the kernel takes the transport's beat and the tick's project frame. Every later frame's beat is the anchor's beat plus whole frames times the tempo. Control ticks fall on absolute multiples of 32 frames, so any block split computes the same positions bit for bit. A step edge within 10⁻⁶ steps after a frame belongs to that frame, so tick rounding never moves an edge.
   - A tempo change re-anchors. A beat more than 10⁻³ beats from the prediction (a seek or a loop across a tempo change) or a project frame other than the next (a loop) restarts the pattern at the new place. So does a transport-generation change, which also clears captures. Each restart's first edge fades from the gain at that moment.
   - Chance rolls are a pure function of (seed, pattern count from the song start, step index before Rotate): splitmix64, top 53 bits. A 60 % step plays the same bars on every playback and in every export. *Re-seed* steps the seed by a fixed stride through 1–9999, so the same seed always re-seeds to the same next one.
   - With no tempo (`beats_per_frame` 0) there is no grid, and Chop fades to the input.
7. **Telemetry for the panel:**
   - the playhead in the pattern;
   - the pattern step playing, and its slot;
   - the pattern's length in beats and its count;
   - whether each step plays this time round;
   - the last whole pattern of the input and of the output as heard (256 peaks each), and the gate's gain (256 means).
8. **Presets** as the design draws them: Init (every step open, so Chop is the input bit for bit), Off-beat chop, Rolling 16ths, Minimal stutter, Dub stab, Triplet roll and Glitch fill (Gate + repeat, the last beat stuttering 2, 4, 4 and 8 times, 5 semitones down). Each draws one bar; steps 17–32 repeat it.
9. **The panel** follows the design in the device-kit style (graphite with the coral blade):
   - mode, steps and division segments, with a readout of the control last touched and the status badge;
   - the preset chips and Re-seed;
   - the pattern view: the input in slate with what is heard in coral, the gate curve, repeat marks, skipped steps shaded and the playhead;
   - five lanes to draw (Level, Length, Ratchet blocks in amber, Repeat in violet, Chance in teal). A stroke is one gesture (begin, updates, commit), so one undo step, and fills the cells it skips along a straight line;
   - knobs for Smooth, Shape, Swing (shown 50–75 %), Rotate, Repeat Pitch, Repeat Decay, Depth, Mix and Seed.

   Other panels have no A/B compare, so Chop has none; Mix at 0 is the dry signal.

## Parameters

Ids are persisted and automated; never rename or reuse one.

| Id | Range | Default | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `chop.mode` | Gate, Repeat, Gate + repeat | Gate | Fades over Smooth. |
| `chop.steps` | 16, 32 | 16 | Pattern length in steps. |
| `chop.division` | 1/8, 1/16, 1/32 | 1/16 | Step length. |
| `chop.smooth` | 0.5–10 ms | 2.9 ms | Every edge's fade; repeat crossfades (≤ half a slice). |
| `chop.shape` | 0–1 | 0.35 | Release Hard → Punch → Soft: length and curve. |
| `chop.swing` | 0–1 | 0 | 50 %–75 %: even steps up to half a step late. |
| `chop.rotate` | 0–31 steps | 0 | Taken modulo the pattern length. |
| `chop.repeat_pitch` | 0–12 st | 0 | The last repeat in a step is this far down. |
| `chop.repeat_decay` | 0–1 | 0 | The last repeat in a step is this much quieter. |
| `chop.depth` | 0–1 | 1 | How far a closed step goes. |
| `chop.mix` | 0–1 | 1 | Dry to chopped. |
| `chop.seed` | 1–9999 | 7 | Chance rolls. |
| `chop.step.<n>.level` | 0–1 | 1 | n = 1..32. |
| `chop.step.<n>.length` | 0–1 | 1 | Of the step, or of each ratchet. |
| `chop.step.<n>.ratchet` | 1–4 | 1 | Hits in the step. |
| `chop.step.<n>.repeat` | 0–3 | 0 | Off, 2, 4, 8 repeats of the first 1/2, 1/4, 1/8. |
| `chop.step.<n>.chance` | 0–1 | 1 | No glide, so the threshold is exact. |

172 parameters: 12 global and 5 × 32 per step.

## Consequences

- The browser lists Chop under Dynamics as "Gate & stutter"; searching gate, stutter, chop, trance, repeat, glitch or ratchet finds it.
- An effect can now keep time by itself from the transport alone. The anchor (position from whole frames since a tick) is the pattern for later step-based effects that must render identically in any block split.
- **Not yet done:** a panel A/B compare across effects; per-step pitch for the gate; triplet divisions.
