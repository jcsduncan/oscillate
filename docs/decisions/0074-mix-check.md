# ADR-0074 — Mix Check: offline checks, blame and one-step fixes

**Status:** Accepted; implemented. **Date:** 30 October 2026.
**Builds on:** ADR-0059 (track renders), ADR-0040 (built-in effects), ADR-0064 (Temper), ADR-0072 (Kick & Bass Lock), spec FS-06 §Mix Check.
**Contract:** `include/oscillate/analysis/mix_check.hpp` (measurements and their thresholds), `include/oscillate/application/mix_check.hpp` (targets, fix rules, findings, `measure`, `report`, `apply`, `judge`, history, reference), `exporting::render_master_taps` (`include/oscillate/export/offline_master.hpp`), `engine::RoutedMixer::node_output`, `ProjectSession::pin_snapshot` / `apply_effect_edits` / `set_audio_clips_fades`, and the Qt `MixCheckController` (`ProjectBridge.mixCheck`). Design: `docs/ui/mix-check-mockup.html`. The command-line runner `tools/mix_check` prints a check of a project.

## Context

Minimal techno and house are often mixed by ear on headphones. The problems that hurt such mixes on a club system are measurable: rumble under the bass, sides that cancel in mono, ringing hats, kick and bass cancelling, peaks over the ceiling, clicks at clip edges, drops that lose energy, and one part hiding another. Measuring one finished project by hand found four of them and fixed each with one device. Mix Check makes that one button, shows the evidence in plain words, blames the track that causes each problem and applies the same fix as one undo step.

## Decision

1. **One render, measured as it plays.** `mix_check::measure` renders a pinned snapshot once (`exporting::render_master_taps`) through the routed mixer and measures it as it renders; nothing is written to disk.
   - **Taps.** After each mixer block the render hands over every audio and instrument track's node output (`RoutedMixer::node_output`, read on the render thread after `mix_block` returns, never on the live callback): post-fader, after the track's inserts and drains, level, pan, gain automation and mute, as it feeds its routes. A latent chain's own delay is taken out, so the tracks line up at one project frame with each other and with the master, which comes from the same render after its chain and level, trimmed like an export with `trim_silent_tail`. A tapped render is always routed, even with nothing on the tracks. Without taps `render_wav_master` is unchanged.
   - **Streaming.** Blocks are gathered into 65 536-frame chunks; each chunk's tracks are measured in parallel, one `analysis::mix::ProfileBuilder` each (fine 16k-point and short 4k-point Hann STFTs, BS.1770 100 ms steps, true peak, sample statistics, the hits' decays and, for audio, each hop's pitch). Silent frames are counted without being transformed, and only hops with sound keep their band powers, so a track that plays five minutes of a 90-minute DJ mix keeps five minutes of hops. Then what needs several tracks: the drum sum's crest (`DrumCrest`: lane energies and cross energies, block peaks), every kick against every non-drum track under the kick's hits (`CancellationMeter`, on `LowBand` lows), and the samples around each unfaded audio clip edge. No song-length signal is kept.
   - Because the taps are post-fader, blame, masking and clipping see each track as it is heard, pan and automation included.
   - **Speed.** A check costs about one offline render of the song: the previous design rendered the master and then each track (`render_track`, which runs the whole project's chains to isolate one), so a 31-second, 8-track song took (tracks + 1) renders, 3.5 minutes. A routed offline render (master, freeze, bounce or check) now runs its instruments and each dependency level of its mixer on worker threads (`AudioWorkerPool::start_owned_threads`, the live default worker count), as live playback does (ADR-0048). Each task writes only its own track's buffers and every node sums its inputs in a fixed order, so the output is bit-identical to a single-threaded render; the unrouted path keeps its serial order.
   - Progress comes from the render (cancel lands within 16 blocks); measuring what is left after it is short.
   - The render takes its lease from a gate of its own, like Freeze's track renders: it never claims a live graph, so playback may start while a check runs. A check refuses to start while playing, exporting or another project operation runs.
   - A re-check measures everything again: one pass renders every track anyway, so reusing unchanged tracks would save nothing.
2. **Checks, thresholds and fixes.** Every value is a named constant in the two headers.

| Check | Measure | Finding from | Blame | Fix (one undo step) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sub rumble | Power under 0.84 × the lowest fundamental against the bass region up to 120 Hz. A bass's lowest fundamental is its lowest played note moved by its sources (Nova's oscillators' octave, semitone and pitch, and its sub on −2/−1/0 octaves; Prism's oscillators and sub; Drift's octave) and by its note effects' downward reach (Scale's octave and transpose, Chord's lowest shift, Echo's falling repeats, Random's range), lowered further when its own spectrum confirms a peak within 50 cents (or half a fine bin) of a played note or one or two octaves under one, no more than 20 dB under its strongest low peak; a bass without notes takes its strongest peak under 120 Hz or a confirmed sub under it; kicks their strongest low peak | 3 dB over a 15 dB allowance ("bad" from 6) | Tracks with ≥ 25 % of the sub power | Contour first: HP 24, cutoff 0.8 × the track's own lowest fundamental (never at or above it), resonance 0, mix 1, output 0; none for a track where that filter cuts the rumble's centre less than 3 dB (too close under the fundamental), and no fix at all when that leaves no track |
| Mono safety | Third-octave fold loss −10 log(mid / (mid + side)) | Span ≥ 2 dB ("bad" from 3) within 40 dB of the loudest band | Most side power in the span | Vista's band covering the span (existing Vista, else a new one last) narrowed so the track loses ≤ 1 dB, net width ≤ 1, ≥ 0.3 |
| Wide lows | Side over mid under 120 Hz | Above −12 dB | Most side power under 120 Hz | Vista Mono Bass on, below 120 Hz |
| Ringing | Narrow peaks of the decay spectrum (hops ≥ 3 dB under the last four) over the ⅓-octave median, judged against the instrument's own sound: each short bin's decay after the hits (a hop 6 dB over the one before starts a hit; power averaged over ≥ 4 hits per offset, followed ≤ 2 s or to the next hit; time to fall 12 dB from its loudest hop) against the energy-weighted median decay of the bins within an octave (its own ±3 bins left out; the whole sound when those hold less than 10 dB under its own energy) | ≥ 9 dB, 300 Hz–16 kHz, within 40 dB of the decay's loudest, and ringing ≥ 3 × as long as the sound around it; not within 50 cents of a played (source-moved) note's first 32 harmonics; for audio, not on the harmonics of the pitch detected in ≥ half its decay hops (a 12-harmonic series from 40 Hz to 1 kHz holding half the power under 5 kHz, with ≥ 3 harmonics each ≥ 5 % of it). Modal partials that decay alike are the sound | The track (any kind) | Temper last, depth 0, a bell cut per peak (≤ 4, the longest-ringing): Q 8, range −9 dB, threshold −80, ratio 20 |
| Kick/bass | Both tracks' lows under the lock crossover (120 Hz, fourth-order Butterworth of (L+R)/2), time-aligned from the taps; under each Anvil hit, 150 ms in 20 ms windows, 10 log((E_kick + E_bass) / E_sum) where the two are within 20 dB of each other and of the hit's loudest window; a hit's dip is its deepest window's | The 90th percentile of the hits' dips ≥ 6 dB (always "warn") | The kick and the bass | Kick & Bass Lock (Keep tone) when the bass is Nova and unlocked |
| Loudness | Integrated, true peak, LRA (EBU Tech 3342: short-term, −70 then −20 LU gates, 10th–95th percentile), drum crest (engine drums plus audio tracks with crest ≥ 14 dB, summed mono) | Outside the target's range ("bad" more than 1 LU out); true peak over the ceiling or the master clipping | — | The master's last enabled Ceiling, only ever lowered: min(current, target − (true peak − current, when positive)) − 0.5 dB, so the overshoot is taken off whether it comes from peaks between samples (Ceiling limits sample peaks and has no true-peak mode) or from gain after it (the master fader, later effects; the finding names which); with none, a new one last at target − 0.5 dB |
| Clicks | Sample jump at each unfaded audio clip edge over the slope's RMS ±2 ms | ≥ 6× and ≥ 0.003 | The track | Fades of 4 ms (smoothstep) on the offending edges, all in one step |
| Energy | Short-term loudness per bar; sections from markers, else 8-bar phrases merged within 2 LU and named by energy | A later drop > 0.5 LU under an earlier one, or a break over a drop | — | None (a note) |
| Masking | Per band over co-active hops (each within 30 dB of its loudest): 10 log(1 + P_other / P_heard), ≥ 4 s together | ≥ 3 dB in bands within 15 dB of the heard track's loudest, above 150 Hz | The less important track (kick, drums and bass, then lead/vox/hook names, then the rest, pads/FX/Ember/Dust last) | Temper last on it: one bell at the span's centre, Q 1.2, range = the loss (≤ 6 dB) |
| Clean-up | Post-fader peaks, L/R per band (the finding names the band), DC, silent tracks | Over full scale; > 1.5 dB; > 0.003; peak under −90 dBFS | The track | None |

   Targets: Club master −9..−7 LUFS, −0.3 dBTP, LRA 4..8, crest 8..12; Streaming −15..−13, −1.0, 5..10, 9..14; Into a DJ mix −12..−9, −2.0, 4..9, 9..14.
3. **Findings.** Each has a stable id (kind + track(s) + band index where the band is part of the problem), severity, plain-words title and text (what, where, how much, why it matters), track chips, band, a small evidence series (spectrum, time or mono), the fix as device, place and parameters, and what to expect. The loudness findings are rebuilt from the same measurements when the target changes; nothing re-renders.
4. **Apply and re-check.** `mix_check::apply` commits one fix as one model `apply`: `apply_effect_edits` adds or edits any number of built-in effects (slot serials allocated in order, as `duck_tracks_to` does), `set_audio_clips_fades` sets every edge's fades, Lock uses `lock_kick_and_bass`. It refuses when the mix differs from the measured snapshot (`same_mix` ignores solo, so Listen does not make a result stale). The controller then re-checks; `judge` reports the target's move ("Sub excess gone", "Mono loss 3.6 → 1.0") and every third-octave band outside the finding's band that moved more than 0.1 dB, and adds the after series to the evidence.
5. **Kick & Bass Lock keeps the kick's tone.** Retuning Anvil's tail changes the kick's character, so `anvil.lock` gained Keep tone (appended: Off 0, Bass note 1, Keep tone 2). Any choice but Off pairs Anvil with Nova; only Bass note retunes. The one-click Lock (and Mix Check's fix) turns kicks on as Keep tone and leaves a kick on Bass note there (ADR-0072 §3, §5).
6. **History.** Each finished check's summary (time, loudness numbers, each finding's id, title and measured amount) is written to `cache/mix-check/history.json` in the project's disposable cache directory, replaced through a temporary file. It is never part of saved project state or the schema; losing it only loses the "since your last check" list, which diffs the previous summary against the new one: fixed, better, worse, new, and "same" for everything within 0.1 dB.
7. **Reference.** A WAV (or, in codec builds, AIFF, FLAC or MP3) of at most 20 minutes is decoded on a worker and profiled; bands Sub, Low, Low-mid, Mid, Presence and Air are compared after matching integrated loudness, and Width as the side-over-mid difference.
8. **Listen** solos the finding's tracks (through the shell's normal solo command) and plays from where the finding is worst (the loudest master hop in its band, the worst hit or the first click); calling it again or `stopListening` stops and restores every solo state.
9. **Determinism.** Offline renders are deterministic for built-in engines; the analyses use no randomness, so the same project gives the same findings, ids and numbers.

## Not done in this version

- Temper's sidechain drives only its whole-spectrum Unmask, so the masking fix is a plain dynamic cut on the less important track, not keyed to the other one.
- Fades use smoothstep, the model's nearest curve to equal power.
- Clip transposes do not exist in the model, so none are applied; a synth sequencing its own notes (Torque) relies on its spectrum.
- The drum crest's peak is exact in blocks where at most four audio candidates sound at once, else a bound (the drums' block peak plus each candidate's).
- Listen's solos are ordinary project edits, so they appear in undo history.

## Consequences

- Tests: `analysis_mix_check` (each measurement on planted signals with hand-derived values: a sub confirmed by the spectrum, a hat's modes that outlast its partials, modal partials that decay alike against one that rings four times longer, streamed kick/bass cancellation and the low band, the streamed drum crest against a direct sum), `application_mix_check_{sub,click,mono,ringing,nova_sub,true_peak,history}` (found, blamed, fixed as one undo step, gone on the re-check; stable ids; a Nova sub an octave down is the bass's lowest fundamental and never rumble; a limiter whose ceiling the peaks run over is lowered by at least the overshoot and the margin; targets; history round trip and diff), `quick_mix_check_controller_contract`, and `native_lock_contract.anvil_keep_tone_locks_without_retuning`.
- The Anvil panel's Lock control needs a third segment, "KEEP TONE".
