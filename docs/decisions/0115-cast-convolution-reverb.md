# ADR-0115 — Cast: a convolution reverb on the native effect platform

**Status:** Accepted and implemented. **Date:** 9 November 2026.
**Supersedes:** ADR-0020 (the opt-in convolution core and its insert kind).
**Builds on:** ADR-0040 (native effect platform), ADR-0043 (one native insert path), ADR-0047 (classic processors as engines), ADR-0058 (effect documents), ADR-0062 (denormals flushed).
**Contract:** `include/oscillate/native_dsp/effects/cast.hpp`, the table `src/native_dsp/effects/cast_table.hpp`, the convolver `src/native_dsp/effects/cast_convolver.hpp`, the IR preparation `src/native_dsp/effects/cast_ir.hpp`; schema 1.48.

## Context

The convolution core of ADR-0020 was its own insert kind behind an opt-in build: type-specific branches in routed playback, offline export and the native insert stage, dual-mono IRs of at most two seconds at four rates, 128-frame calls, and no live playback. Expanse covers algorithmic reverb; a convolution reverb is the other half: real or modelled spaces, plates and springs, played exactly.

## Decision

1. **An effect engine, Cast (id `cast`).** The name: an impulse response is a cast taken of a space. Cast is an ordinary `NativeEffectPatch` on the shared insert path; the `native_convolution` kind, its model type, codec kind, route kind, live admission and every convolution branch are deleted, and so is `OSCILLATE_BUILD_CONVOLUTION_DSP`: Cast ships in every build. Schema 1.48 drops the kind and lets a built-in effect's document hold managed media slots. Opening a 1.47 project turns each convolution insert into a Cast playing the same IR ([ADR-0095](0095-schema-migration-policy.md)).
2. **The IR lives in the document.** Media slot `ir` holds a bundled IR (a builtin name) or a user's file (mono or stereo, any rate the media layer decodes: WAV, FLAC, AIFF). Slot `ir.right`, a second stereo file, makes `ir` the left input's response and itself the right input's: true stereo. The media layer reads mono and stereo files only, so a four-channel true-stereo file is loaded as its two stereo halves. Stages decode managed slots off the callback through `StageContext::media`; a change of managed media rebuilds the stage, while a change between bundled IRs is a live document edit.
3. **Routing.** Mono (one response on each channel), stereo (one per channel) and true stereo (left in to left and right out, right in to left and right out).
4. **Zero-latency partitioned convolution** on the pinned PFFFT real transform. Taps 0–63 run directly in time; uniformly partitioned overlap-save tiers take 64–1,023 (64-frame partitions), 1,024–4,095 (256), 4,096–16,383 (1,024) and 16,384 onwards (4,096). The first tier starts one block in and runs at its block's end; each later tier starts at least two of its blocks in, so it computes one block's product (its input transform, the partition products and the inverse) in steps spread over the next block. The callback's work is even at any callback size and the output is the direct convolution sample for sample, independent of block splits. Silent input stops the products once every partition reads silence. Each kernel holds input history for the longest IR (10 s at the session rate).
5. **The IR group** reshapes the IR off the callback: Start (0–500 ms), Length (5–100 % of what follows, with a 50 ms fade at a cut), Stretch (50–200 %: a phase vocoder, 2048-point Hann frames at 8× overlap, that scales time and keeps pitch; the first 2.5 ms stay unstretched and crossfade in), Decay (10–100 % of the measured RT60, an exponential envelope), Fade In (0–500 ms), Reverse and Normalise (each output receives unit power from uncorrelated unit-power inputs, so IRs and mixes match in level). These are `EffectEngine::document_parameters`: not automatable, compiled with the document on control (a panel drag recompiles), and reusing the previous compile's decoded source and stretched IR when only later stages change. A new IR, or a reshaped one, crossfades in over one block of each tier and 64 frames in the head.
6. **The wet path, live:** Pre-Delay (0–250 ms, Lagrange-interpolated, gliding), Width (0–200 %, mid/side), Low Cut (20 Hz, off, to 2 kHz) and High Cut (1 kHz to 20 kHz, off), both second-order Butterworth, Tilt (±6 dB on two shelves at 1 kHz), Duck (0–30 dB) under its own input or the sidechain with a threshold and release, Wet Gain (−24 to +12 dB) and an equal-power Mix.
7. **Real time.** Compiled IRs are shared between documents and the kernel; the kernel holds the ones it may still read (the head's and each tier's previous and next) and hands each it stops reading to a single-producer ring that the next compile drains on control, so the callback never frees or allocates. Every engine's `prepare_document` now takes the effect's values, its decoded media and the previous state (`DocumentInputs`); the runtime retires every compiled document but the newest, the one the callback adopted and the one it may be adopting.
8. **Tail.** `EffectEngine::document_tail_frames` gives the IR's length after Start, Length and Stretch (capped at 10 s) plus the pre-delay and 50 ms for the tone filters; freeze, bounce and export use it. Latency is 0.
9. **The bundled IRs** are modelled at 48 kHz on first use, resampled to the session rate (Kaiser-windowed sinc, 32 zero crossings, β = 10) and cached for the process; no recorded IRs are shipped, so there is no third-party content or licence beyond the project's own. All are true stereo through an ORTF pair (two cardioids, 17 cm, ±55°).
   - **Rooms** (Vocal Booth, Small Room, Live Room, Echo Chamber, Concert Hall, Cathedral, Warehouse): shoebox image sources to the mixing time (√V ms), each reflection filtered by its wall absorption per octave band and air absorption (ISO 9613-1) for its distance, then a diffuse tail of shaped Gaussian noise whose every bin decays at the band's Eyring RT60, at the level the diffuse field has for the room's volume, fading in over the second half of the mixing time with the early sound fading out. The pair's coherence follows the diffuse-field sinc for its spacing.
   - **Plates** (Plate, Bright Plate): a dense field from the start, decaying per band like an EMT 140, brighter towards the top, with the lows arriving last (dispersion) and decorrelated pickups.
   - **Spring**: four springs (one per response) as a feedback loop of 128 first-order allpasses (coefficient −0.7, so each echo is a falling chirp), a 5 kHz damping pole and the tank's 80 Hz–4.5 kHz output band.
10. **Telemetry:** input and wet peaks per channel, the ducking, the shaped IR's length, routing and RT60, and its envelope and energy decay curve over 128 points.
11. **Panel** (`qml/cast`): the IR browser (bundled IRs by category, Load, Load Right for true stereo, and files dropped on the panel) over the IR's envelope and decay curve with its length and RT60; the IR group; pre-delay and width; the tone section with its response; ducking; wet gain and mix; the input and wet meters.

## Consequences

- **Tests:** `native_cast_contract` (every routing against a double-precision direct FIR across every tier boundary at 64, 128 and irregular blocks, zero latency, true stereo keeps the inputs apart, bit-exact block splits and export against live, each bundled IR's measured RT60 and decorrelated tail, stretch and decay against RT60, start, length and reverse, normalised levels, pre-delay, tone and width, ducking, the equal-power mix, a click-free IR swap, the reported tail, live recompiles, a resampled file, hostile input, restrained presets, no allocation while adopting a new IR and seeking, telemetry) and `quick_cast_panel_contract`. `effect_export_parity` and the platform contracts cover it with every engine; `native_effect_stage_contract`, `insert_schema` and `application_project_session` cover managed effect media.
- **CPU** (i7-10700K, Release, one pinned core, 10 s of stereo noise at 48 kHz, true-stereo bundled IRs of 0.4–9.5 s): 1.2–1.9 % of real time at 64 and 128 frames; the 99th-percentile block takes 2.3–5.2 % of its period and the longest 3.2–12.3 %. See [native effects CPU](../performance/native-effects.md).
- **Memory:** about 8 MB of input history per instance at 48 kHz (twice that at 96 kHz), plus the compiled IR (its spectra: twice its taps per response).
