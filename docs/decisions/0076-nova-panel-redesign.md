# ADR-0076 — Nova panel: resize and reflow, a signal spine, a browser that lets you play

**Status:** Accepted; implemented, including the first revision (decisions 8–12) and revision 2 (decisions 13–19, design [ui/nova-overview-mockup.html](../ui/nova-overview-mockup.html)), which supersedes decisions 4 and 8–9 for page 0, the spine and the header. **Date:** 31 October 2026.
**Builds on:** ADR-0054 (UI update architecture), ADR-0057 (Nova), ADR-0073 (Nova Analogue).
**Panel design:** [ui/nova-redesign-mockup.html](../ui/nova-redesign-mockup.html). **Next:** ADR-0077 (✦ Constellation) fills the place this ADR reserves for it.

## Context

The Nova panel was a large blue card that was hard to navigate. It could not be resized, the preset explorer was hard to use, and the explorer captured the typing keyboard, so keys typed into it instead of playing notes.

- The instrument window drew every panel at a fixed designed size, scaled to fit and capped at 82 %, so Nova (1360 × 880) always drew small, and the window could not be resized.
- Six page tabs hid where each part sits in the signal.
- The preset browser covered the whole panel, and opening it put the cursor in its search box. The typing keyboard yields to any focused text field, so letters typed instead of playing notes.
- The navy plates turned every card into one blue mass.

## Decision

1. **A resizable window.** `FloatingDeviceFrame` gains an opt-in `resizable`: a grip at the bottom-right corner, plus grabs along the right and bottom edges. A drag asks the host for a size (`resizeRequested`) between the frame's minimum and the window's edge. The surface stays put while its size changes. `NativeSynthEditorOverlay` turns it on while floating. Fixed-size panels then scale to fill the window, without the 82 % cap; that cap stays only for a window never resized.
2. **Nova reflows.** For Nova the overlay lays the panel out at the window's area divided by the zoom: `scale = min(zoom, area / minimum)` and `size = area / scale`.
   - Above its minimum (1180 × 720) the panel is drawn at exactly the zoom and gains room, not magnification. Below it, the whole panel scales down.
   - Each page declares its own `minimumWidth`/`minimumHeight`: the size its cards need without overlapping. A page given less is laid out at that minimum and scaled to fit the area, so the header and spine never shrink with it.
   - Above its minimum, each page shares out the extra space. On the OSC page the oscillator and filter rows grow, and the oscillator display and filter response with them. Global's rows and meters grow, the Voice Lanes grow, and the step pattern and matrix widen.
3. **Zoom chips** (85, 100, 120 and 140 %) sit in the Nova header. The window size and the zoom persist in the workspace preferences (`WorkspaceLayoutController`, QSettings `workspace/instrumentWindowWidth`, `…Height`, `workspace/novaZoom`). Docked mode is unchanged: it fits the dock at up to 100 %.
4. **A signal spine replaces the page tabs.** Under the header, tiles run OSC A → OSC B → OSC C → SUB · NOISE → FILTER 1 → FILTER 2 → FX → OUT, with VOICES beside the path. Each tile shows:
   - an LED from the module's on switch;
   - a live thumbnail: the oscillator's cycle (telemetry while sounding), the filter's response at its modulated cutoff, the output level, or a sketch;
   - a sub-label: table or shape, model and cutoff, the Main chain's module names, Analogue's age.

   A tile opens the page that holds its module: FX → FX, OUT → GLOBAL, VOICES → VOICES. On the OSC page it also sets `focusModule`, so that card glows in its accent and a focused oscillator takes 40 % of the oscillator row while the other two keep their minimum width. MATRIX, ARP · CLIP and GLOBAL are compact tabs beside the spine. `panel.page` keeps its meaning.
5. **The browser lets you play.** The preset browser becomes a drawer docked left of the pages (BROWSE toggles it; the pages narrow beside it). It opens with its list focused: a ListView, not a text field, so the typing keyboard plays notes, and ↑/↓ move and load. "/" or a click enters the search; Escape there returns to the list; Return or Escape in the list closes. A header pill shows which: green KEYS PLAY NOTES, or amber TYPING IN SEARCH · ESC TO PLAY (or TYPING A NAME while the save field has the keys). Rows show their category's colour and their tags. Search, chips, favourites and the user-preset footer are kept.
6. **Graphite.** `NovaTheme` moves from navy plates to graphite neutrals (window `#0b0e15`, rail `#080a10`, card `#11151f`, `#161b28`, well `#06080d`, lines `#1d2330` and `#2a3242`). Cyan, violet and amber stay the only value colours. Pink and green mark only the spine's FX and SUB · NOISE tiles; the category colours mark only presets. No Nova QML file hard-codes a plate colour; each uses a theme token.
7. **Constellation's place.** A `✦ CONSTELLATION` header button toggles `panel.constellationOpen`. That shows `novaConstellationHost`, over the browser and pages, for ADR-0077's view. Opening it closes the browser, and a spine tile or tab closes it.

## Revision: match the design, and no blue mass (31 October 2026)

The first build diverged from the design and still read mostly blue. The OSC page kept all seven cards at once with small knobs; the header carried the readout, voices, CPU, master and status; the greys still leaned blue; and every arc, toggle and glow was cyan. This revision supersedes decision 4's OSC-page focus and decision 6's tokens.

8. **A focus view replaces the OSC page.** Page 0 shows whatever `panel.focusModule` names, large: a spine module (`a`, `b`, `c`, `subnoise`, `filter1`, `filter2`, `out`), a modulation source (`env.1`–`env.4`, `lfo.1`–`lfo.8`) or `macros`.
   - **Display card** (left, 60 %): the wavetable's 3D frames (`NovaOscDisplay`), the filter response (`NovaFilterResponse`, still draggable), the sub's shape over the noise (`NovaSubNoiseDisplay`), the envelope (`NovaEnvelopeView`), the LFO shape (`NovaCurveEditor`), the eight macros big (`NovaMacros`, full width), or the output's meters. Titled "OSC A · WAVETABLE", "FILTER 1 · RESPONSE", "ENV 1 · AMP", with the sub-label in the module's accent on the right.
   - **CONTROLS card** (right, 40 %): every control the module has, flowing in a roomy grid with full-size knobs and readable captions (`NovaOscControls`, `NovaFilterControls` with the routing matrix `NovaRouting`, `NovaSubNoiseControls`, `NovaSourceControls`, and for OUT the master knob and a way to GLOBAL). It scrolls when shorter than its contents, so nothing is unreachable. Below 900 px the controls go under the display.
   - **Related cards:** three compact cards with live thumbnails; a click focuses one. An oscillator shows SUB · NOISE, FILTER 1, VOICES; SUB · NOISE shows OSC A, FILTER 1, OUT; a filter shows OSC A, SUB · NOISE or FILTER 1, and FX; OUT shows FX, VOICES, OSC A; sources show OSC A, FILTER 1, FX.
   - **MOD SOURCES rail:** a chip per source (ENV 1 · AMP … ENV 4 in amber; LFO 1–8, the performance and voice sources, and the eight macros in violet), "DRAG ONTO ANY KNOB · CLICK TO EDIT" on the right. Dragging a chip routes it at 30 % as the ✥ handles do; clicking an envelope or LFO focuses it, a macro focuses the macros. The modulation strip, its tabs and `panel.modTab` are gone.
9. **Spine and header as the design.** Tiles are 74 px tall with a 35 px thumbnail, title and LED on top and the sub-label below, in the order OSC A, B, C, SUB · NOISE, FILTER 1, FILTER 2, FX, then VOICES and OUT. OUT opens the focus view on the output. The header holds only the wordmark, one preset box (◀ name CATEGORY, EDITED ▶), BROWSE, ✦ CONSTELLATION, the keys pill and zoom. The TOUCH A CONTROL readout, voices, CPU and the Live badge move to a slim status line above the keys; the master knob moves to OUT's controls.
10. **Browser as the design.** The chip row holds the categories (ALL, BASS, LEAD …) and ★ favourites, filled in their colour when on; the tag chips wait behind a #TAGS chip and tags are searched as text. Rows are roomier: name, category and ★, with the tags on a line beneath; the footer is compact.
11. **A playable key strip** runs along the bottom: ten white keys labelled A S D F G H J K L ; and the black keys W E T Y U O P, from the typing keyboard's octave (`keyBase`, from `computerKeyboard.octave`; C3 when there is none). A press reports `keyPressed(note, velocity)` (louder nearer a key's foot), held until `keyReleased(note)`; the overlay plays them on the synth's track as Crate's pads do. A key lights while held and while a voice plays it (telemetry `T.voiceKey`).
12. **Neutral graphite, an accent per module.** `NovaTheme` drops the blue from every neutral: bg `#0a0a0b`, plate `#0f1011`, rail `#0c0c0d`, card `#151618`, card2 `#1b1c1f`, well `#08090a`, lines `#232427` / `#303236`, text `#ececee`, dim `#9a9ca3`, faint `#63656c`, with neutral knob bodies, grid lines, hovers and selection. Colour now says which module a control belongs to, matching its spine tile: oscillators cyan, SUB · NOISE green, filters amber, FX pink, VOICES violet, envelopes amber, LFOs and the other sources violet, OUT and global settings neutral white. `NovaPanel.accentFor(id)` maps a parameter id to its module's accent, and `NovaKnob`, `NovaLed`, `NovaSegs`, `NovaPill` and `NovaDropdown` take their `accent` from it unless one is set; displays, focus glows and selected segments follow. The NOVA wordmark and the ✦ CONSTELLATION sweep keep the brand's cyan-violet-pink. Nova's floating window wears the same graphite: `FloatingDeviceFrame` gains `titleBarColor` and button colours, and the overlay binds them (and the frame and border) to Nova's theme while Nova is open; other engines keep their look. No Nova QML file hard-codes a blue.

## Consequences

- Nova is legible at any window size the user picks. Text grows only through the zoom chips, never by stretching a fixed layout.
- A page that cannot fit (the focus view under 640 × 460, the VOICES page under 1100 × 792) still scales. That is the honest floor of its cards' current contents; reflowing below it would mean redesigning the cards.
- The test harnesses load the panel at its designed size (1360 × 932). The overlay's designed-size table no longer lists Nova; it reads the panel's own.
- Tests: the browser's focus model (list first, "/", Escape) and its tags behind #TAGS; the spine's pages, the focus view's displays, controls and related cards, source chips focusing and dragging, and the LEDs; zoom and constellation; page reflow, the narrow layout and scale-down; the key strip's notes, velocities and lights; and, in the app, the grip resizing the window with Nova reflowing at the same scale, the zoom chips scaling it, both persisted, and Nova's graphite chrome. Tests that touch a module's controls focus it first.

## Revision 2: the whole synth on one page (31 October 2026)

The focus view's colours, graphite, key strip, status line and browser worked, but most controls belonged back on the main panel. Showing one module at a time hid what drives what: tweaking a filter meant losing sight of the oscillators feeding it and the envelope moving it, and every module was a click away. The tall spine tiles took 92 px for what a thin strip says, and the related cards duplicated it. A second design ([ui/nova-overview-mockup.html](../ui/nova-overview-mockup.html)) settles on: a slim strip instead of tall tiles; every control visible, with hierarchy rather than hiding; an inline envelope and LFO editor; and the overview by default, with a module opened large only on request. This revision supersedes decision 4's spine, decision 8's focus view and decision 9's spine; the colours (decision 12), the browser (10) and the key strip (11) stay.

13. **MAIN is an overview.** Page 0 (`NovaOverviewPage`) holds a card per module in the design's grid: OSC A, OSC B, OSC C and SUB · NOISE across the top (the fourth column narrower, weights 1 : 1 : 1 : 0.62); FILTER 1, FILTER 2 and ROUTING (two columns wide) under them; MODULATION (three columns) and MACROS at the foot. A row is as tall as its tallest card, and spare height is shared between the rows and goes to each card's display. Below `overviewNarrowWidth` (1050 px of page area, margins included) the grid has two columns: OSC A, OSC B / OSC C, SUB · NOISE / FILTER 1, FILTER 2 / ROUTING / MODULATION / MACROS. When the cards need more height than the page has, it scrolls (a vertical Flickable) rather than scaling; it scales only below 640 px wide.
14. **One card, hierarchy not hiding.** `NovaModuleCard` is every card's frame: a header with the module's on LED, a bold mono title, its type dropdown (the oscillator type, the filter model), a sub-label in the module's accent on the right (what an oscillator plays, a filter's cutoff) and ⤢; then its display (`NovaOscDisplay`, `NovaFilterResponse`, `NovaSubNoiseDisplay`) and its controls. The controls components from the focus view are reused, not duplicated, and now lay themselves out in two tiers: the PRIMARY knobs at full size (LEVEL, the type's main knobs and PAN; CUTOFF, RESONANCE, DRIVE, FAT; SUB, OCT, NOISE, AM), then under a rule the SECONDARY controls with small knobs in `NovaKnobGroup`s, each under a small label turned on its side (UNISON with its curve and stack, WARP, GRAIN, SAMPLE, SPECTRAL, PITCH; a filter's KEYTRACK, PAN and MIX; the noise's PITCH, TONE, PAN and the AM curve and source). A warp slot's mode sits under its amount. Every parameter stays visible on MAIN without opening anything; the every-parameter test sweeps only the oscillator types and the selected modulation source. ROUTING carries the routing grid and the master level (OUT); the macros' card shows the eight macros at full size with their names and ✥ handles.
15. **Off modules fade.** A card whose module is off (`moduleOn`) drops to `offOpacity` (0.42) and rises to 0.75 under the pointer, so the active signal path reads at a glance. Per-module accents (`accentFor`) are unchanged.
16. **Expand in place.** ⤢, or a double-click on a card's title, opens that card large over the overview (`panel.focusModule`, "" when none) with a scrim behind it and a bigger display; the other cards keep their places under the scrim. Esc, ⤢ again or a click on the scrim restores it. While a card is large, only its knobs take a dropped source. The oscillators, SUB · NOISE, the filters and MODULATION open; ROUTING and MACROS have nothing more to show. `NovaFocusPage` and `NovaFocusCard` are removed.
17. **MODULATION.** A row of source tabs (`NovaSourceChip`): ENV 1 · AMP to ENV 4 in amber, LFO 1–8, the performance and voice sources and the macros in violet, each with its route count raised. A click selects an envelope or LFO (`panel.modTab`, the amp envelope at first): its editor (`NovaEnvelopeView` or `NovaCurveEditor`) fills the left of the card and its knobs (`NovaSourceControls`) the right, the stages or timing at full size, the curves and velocity small. A macro's tab jumps to the MACROS card; a drag from any tab onto any knob routes it at 30 % as before. "CLICK TO EDIT · DRAG ONTO ANY KNOB" sits on the right of the header.
18. **A slim strip.** The 92 px spine becomes a 28 px strip: per module a light (grey while off), its mono name and a small live thumbnail (about 3.4 × 1.2 of the 12 px type), with › between: OSC A, OSC B, OSC C, SUB · NOISE, FILTER 1, FILTER 2, FX, VOICES, OUT. A sound module's item shows MAIN, scrolls to its card and flashes it with a glow; FX, VOICES and OUT open the FX, VOICES and GLOBAL pages.
19. **Header and sizes.** The header reads: wordmark, preset box, BROWSE, ✦ CONSTELLATION, page buttons (MAIN, FX, MATRIX, ARP · CLIP, VOICES, GLOBAL; `novaTab:N` follows `panel.page`), the keys pill and zoom. Narrower than 1250 px it is compact: ✦ alone and the keys pill as its light. The status line and key strip are unchanged. The designed size becomes 1440 × 1240 so the overview fits unscaled and unscrolled; the minimum becomes 980 × 720 (the compact header), so the narrow layout can be reached.

Consequences of revision 2: tests drive `focusModule` only to open a card large and `modTab` to select a source; they lay out the source tabs' Flow before reading its positions. The FX, MATRIX, ARP · CLIP, VOICES and GLOBAL pages are unchanged. The harnesses load the panel at 1440 × 1240.
